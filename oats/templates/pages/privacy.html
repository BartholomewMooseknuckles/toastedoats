Privacy

Short, because there's not much.

Last updated {{ site.legal.updated }}

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your email address, and if you reserve a box, your name, ZIP code, flavor picks and any notes. If you message us, the message. That's it.

What we don't

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties - no analytics, no pixels, no ad tags. Your box is kept in your own browser's storage until you reserve it. We do not store IP addresses or device fingerprints.

What we do with it

We email you about the thing you asked about: the waitlist, your pre-order, the newsletter if you joined it. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and we never will.

Your choices

Email {{ site.contact.email }} from the address in question and we'll delete everything we have about you, usually the same day. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, so there's nothing to opt out of, but the request above works all the same.

Where it lives

On a server we run, in the United States, behind Cloudflare. Cloudflare sees the traffic the way any network provider would; see their privacy policy for how they handle it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and we'll say so in the next email.
