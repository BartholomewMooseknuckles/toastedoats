Accessibility

Everyone eats.

We build this site to WCAG 2.2 AA: real headings, labelled forms, keyboard access to everything including the cart, visible focus, text that scales, colour contrast of at least 4.5:1 on every flavor theme, and animation that switches off when your system asks for reduced motion.

Images of food are described in words. Prices are text, not pictures. The site works without JavaScript except for building a box, which falls back to the waitlist.

If something is hard to use, tell us: {{ site.contact.email }}. Say which page and what happened and we'll fix it, and reply to you when we have.
