The name

Why toasted?

Because they are.

We toast the oats and we toast the hemp hearts before anything gets milled. Toasting brings out warm, nutty, roasted-cereal notes that raw oats just don't have - it's why the base tastes like something instead of tasting like fuel.

We toast the oats.
We toast the hemp.
You can connect the dots.
