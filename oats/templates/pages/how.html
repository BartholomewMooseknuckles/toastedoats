Good to know

The small print, big.

Any milk

Dairy, oat, almond, soy - about 2/3 cup (140-180 mL). Less milk, thicker pudding. We like it thick.

Jar or bowl

A jar lets you shake instead of stir and takes the whole thing to work. A bowl is a bowl.

Overnight means 6+ hours

Four in a pinch. It's edible after one, but it's oatmeal after one. Give it the night.

Swirl last-ish, crunch last

The swirl can go in the night before if you want it to bleed through. The crunch never can. That's why it's in its own packet.

Three days

Once it's made, eat it within three days. It won't last that long.

Warm it?

You can. Thirty seconds in the microwave turns it into something between pudding and a baked oat. Still add the crunch after.
