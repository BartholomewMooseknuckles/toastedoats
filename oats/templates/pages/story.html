Our story

Dessert energy.
Breakfast macros.

We wanted something that looks like you shouldn't be allowed to eat it while watching your macros. Then we made it out of oats.

Pudding, not oatmeal.

Overnight oats are a great idea with a texture problem. Soaked whole oats stay loose and a little slimy, and the toppings sink. So we don't use whole oats. The Sweet Cream Base is finely milled toasted oats, toasted hemp hearts and chia - it hydrates into something thick and smooth, closer to a frozen-custard base than a bowl of porridge.

Then it gets treated like a custard base: an intensely flavored swirl, a separate crunch, piled on right before you eat. Greek-yogurt mix-ins, pudding, overnight oats and the frozen-custard flavor board, all in one jar.

One base. Seven vibes.

Rather than engineer a different nutritional foundation for every flavor, every pouch starts from the same base. Flavors come from three small, loud things: a dry flavor modifier in the base (cocoa, peanut, cake), a swirl (strawberry, blueberry, fudge, frosting) and a crunch (shortcake crumbs, cobbler crumble, brownie chunks, cookie rubble). Small amounts, dramatic results, same macros underneath. It's how a good custard shop works, and it's why we can keep dropping flavors without touching the formula.

Why "toasted"?

Literally, because we toast the oats and we lightly toast the hemp hearts before milling. Toasting is what gives the base its warm, nutty, roasted-cereal flavor; raw oats taste like the box they came in.

Culturally, you know. We know you know. The joke works because everything else about this brand is serious: the ingredients, the texture, the photography, the macros. No leaves on the pack, no fake dispensary graphics, nothing in the pouch but food. A food brand that a certain culture adopts - not a novelty product pretending to be food.

What it isn't.

  • It isn't a supplement. Taste first, then texture, then satiety, then nutrition. In that order.
  • It isn't "functional". No adaptogens, no mushrooms, no terpenes, no cannabis. Oats, hemp hearts, chia, protein, fiber, real flavor.
  • It isn't done yet. We're locking the final formula with our co-packer now, which is why the macros on this site are words, not numbers. Numbers come when they're real.
