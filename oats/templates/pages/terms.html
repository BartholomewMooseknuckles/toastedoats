Pre-orders

A reservation is a request, not a purchase. No payment is taken when you reserve. When product is ready to ship we will email you a checkout link with the final price, which may differ from the pre-order pricing shown on this site. You can ignore that email; if you do, the reservation lapses and nothing happens. Reservations don't guarantee availability of a particular flavor.

Subscriptions

When subscriptions go live, each order is charged when it ships, you'll get an email before every renewal, and you can skip, swap flavors or cancel any time from the link in that email. No fees, no phone calls.
