The ritual

Make it.
Swirl it.
Crunch it.

Five steps. Four of them are "wait". The last one is the good part.

  1. {% endfor %}
  1. 01

    Pour

    Start with the Sweet Cream Base: finely milled toasted oats, toasted hemp hearts and chia. Not a bag of loose oats.

  2. 02

    Add milk

    About two-thirds of a cup of whatever milk you drink. Stir it, or shake it if you're in a jar. Ten seconds.

  3. 03

    Set overnight

    Go to bed. Wake up to thick, creamy oat pudding. Not oatmeal. Pudding.

  4. 04

    Swirl

    Squeeze in the flavor swirl. Ribbons, not a mix - leave it marbled. That's where the big flavor lives.

  5. 05

    Crunch

    Tear open the crunch and pile it on. It ships in its own packet so it's never, ever soggy.
